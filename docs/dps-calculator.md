# Native P99 DPS Calculator

The DPS Calculator is a native, offline app page under **Combat > DPS Calculator**. It does not embed or frame the external calculator.

## Inputs

The calculator accepts manual race, class, level, strength, haste, skill, and weapon values. Race and class choices are limited to valid combinations. Choosing either one loads the Project 1999 creation values for Strength, Stamina, Agility, Dexterity, Wisdom, Intelligence, Charisma, and unallocated bonus points; the values remain editable.

Primary and secondary weapons can be selected directly from the shared Wardrobe item catalog. The chooser is filtered by equipment slot, race, and class, and selecting a weapon fills damage, delay, and handedness. A two-handed main weapon disables and clears the offhand calculation.

The calculator can also populate its values from either:

- a saved character's equipped inventory and profile; or
- the current Wardrobe draft.

Imported values remain editable. Equipped strength is added to the entered base strength, and worn haste uses the highest equipped haste value.

## Output

The page shows main-hand, offhand, and estimated total DPS, plus effective delays, damage ranges, level/class weapon caps, damage bonus, damage modifier, haste cap, attack chances, and an informational maximum backstab value.

The estimate excludes misses, target AC, attack rating, disciplines, and proc damage. Backstab is displayed but is not added to total DPS.

## Implementation and provenance

The formulas are implemented locally in `src/app/dps-calculator/model.ts`, equipment loading is isolated in `equipment.ts`, and the two-hand damage-bonus lookup is bundled in `twoHandBonus.ts`. The starting-stat lookup is generated by `tools/scrape_starting_stats.py` from the Project 1999 class tables and checked into the app, so there is no runtime wiki request.

This is a native TypeScript adaptation of the reusable [jklein.me EQ DPS Calculator](https://www.jklein.me/eqcalc/) source. The original DOM/global-state implementation was converted into a typed, pure calculation model and integrated with saved-character and Wardrobe equipment. The app keeps the published mechanics and factual two-hand damage-bonus lookup while adding explicit weapon handedness and enforcing the level/class damage caps shown by the calculator.
